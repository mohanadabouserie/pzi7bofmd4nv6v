The 1 October 2026 change lands in 10 days

Service messages — every free-form reply the bot sends inside the 24-hour window — have been free since November 2024. From 1 Oct 2026 Meta charges them at each country's utility rate, with 1,000 free per number per month and no volume discount. Egypt's rate is $0.0036 per message.

At your current setting that adds $14.61 a month.

Inbound messages stay free and voice costs Meta nothing extra — only the bot's text reply is billed. The free grant is per number, so a second number brings another 1,000.

Why voice costs more

Show the reasons
  • Each note takes one extra short call to write it down; the audio in it bills at 32 tokens/second and double the text rate.
  • After that it is answered exactly like a typed question, and the off-topic guard screens it too.
  • Voice answers are never cached.
  • Only about +16% at 15s and +45% at the 45s cap. It is not the thing to worry about.
